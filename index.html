How to bridge
Ethereum to Optimism

A deposit starts on Ethereum and arrives in your wallet on OP Mainnet. The Standard Bridge relays the deposit message between the two networks.

Start an Optimism Bridge transfer ↗

  1. Choose the asset and route

    Set Ethereum as the source and Optimism as the destination. Enter the amount, check the receiving token and confirm the recipient address.

  2. Review gas and token allowance

    Keep enough ETH for the deposit. For ERC-20 tokens, review the spender and approve the required amount when your existing allowance is insufficient.

  3. Confirm the deposit in your wallet

    Review the transaction and sign on Ethereum. Save the transaction hash so you can follow its inclusion and the destination delivery.

  4. Use your assets on OP Mainnet

    Switch your wallet to Optimism and check the balance. Import the verified token contract if the asset is not yet visible in your wallet.

Optimism Bridge
FAQ

Practical answers about deposits, fees, token versions and withdrawals.

What is Optimism Bridge?

Optimism Bridge moves ETH and supported ERC-20 tokens between Ethereum and Optimism, also called OP Mainnet. Choose a token and route, review the destination asset and fees, then confirm the transfer in your wallet.

How long does bridging to Optimism take?

Standard Bridge deposits from Ethereum to Optimism usually take 1-3 minutes. Ethereum congestion and transaction inclusion affect arrival time. A canonical withdrawal back to Ethereum includes a seven-day challenge period after the withdrawal is proven, followed by finalization.

Do I need ETH to bridge USDC or USDT?

Yes. Keep ETH on Ethereum to pay for the token approval and deposit transaction. Keep ETH on Optimism for transactions after arrival. OP is the governance token; ETH pays transaction fees on OP Mainnet.

Will I receive USDC or USDC.e on Optimism?

The destination token depends on the route. A Standard Bridge transfer of Ethereum USDC delivers bridged USDC.e. A Circle CCTP route burns native USDC on Ethereum and mints native USDC on Optimism. Match the destination token contract to the app where you intend to use it.

Why is the fee different for ETH and ERC-20 tokens?

An ETH deposit has no token-allowance transaction. An ERC-20 deposit may need a separate approval when the existing allowance is too low. Each transaction consumes gas, and the final charge uses gas consumed multiplied by its effective gas price. Route fees can add to the total.

Can I bridge from Optimism back to Ethereum?

Yes. A canonical withdrawal involves initiating the withdrawal on Optimism, proving it on Ethereum, waiting for the seven-day challenge period, and finalizing it on Ethereum. Liquidity routes can provide an earlier payout, with fees and availability determined by the route.

What should I check if my deposit is pending?

Check the Ethereum transaction receipt first. A pending source transaction needs inclusion before the deposit can progress. After confirmation, check the recipient address, switch your wallet to OP Mainnet, and verify the destination token contract. Use the original transaction hash to track the transfer before sending again.
